Homes, water heaters and sewer lines

The village has about 7,450 housing units, and a house is only as reliable as its oldest pipe. Check the water heater's data plate for a build date. A tank that is past its usual service life and leaking at the base needs water heater replacement, because a patch will not stop a corroded steel shell.

If more than one drain in the house runs slow, suspect the main sewer line rather than a single fixture. Tree roots enter at joints, and cast iron or clay pipe can sag and hold water. A sewer inspection shows the real condition on video.

Before We Arrive in Matteson

Find the main valve

Most Matteson homes have the main shutoff near where the water line enters, often by the meter or the water heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If the handle will not budge, leave it and tell us when we pick up.

Kill the power near water

Water and electricity do not mix. If a ceiling is dripping or the basement floor is wet, switch off the breakers for that area from a dry spot. Never wade into standing water to reach the panel.

Clear a path

Move rugs, boxes and anything valuable out of the splash zone. Shop owners along the retail strip should also pull stock off low shelves and tell us how the back door opens, since deliveries often use it.
