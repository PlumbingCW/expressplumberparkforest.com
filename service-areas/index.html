Service Areas Around Park Forest, IL

Our licensed plumbers answer calls from Park Forest and five neighboring towns, plus several nearby communities reached by phone.

Call (855) 575-2890

We are based at 200 Main St in Park Forest, and our 24/7 phone line sends plumbers to the villages around us. Each town below has its own page with local roads, water supply facts and what to prepare before a technician arrives.

How the towns line up around us

To the west and north, we serve Matteson, Richton Park and Olympia Fields. To the south and east, we serve University Park and Chicago Heights. Distances are short, but we do not promise arrival times because traffic and active calls vary.

What each town page covers

Matteson, ZIP 60443, sits west of Park Forest along US 30, with I-57 and Cicero Avenue close by. The village has about 7,450 housing units, so its page covers storefronts as well as houses. If water quality is your question, ask the Village which supplier serves your address.

Richton Park, ZIP 60471, draws its drinking water from village wells and sends wastewater to the Metropolitan Water Reclamation District. The Village maintains 42 miles of sewer line. That matters on a stormy night, because a backup can start in the street main or in your own line, and the page explains how to tell the two apart.

Olympia Fields, ZIP 60461, is a small village with about 1,900 housing units, and permanent homes there go back to around 1919. Older houses can still carry older pipe, so the page walks through what each era of plumbing tends to do and when a repipe is worth pricing.

University Park, ZIP 60484, buys its water from Aqua Illinois, a private utility. Questions about taste, color or lead start with Aqua Illinois, and a lab test tells you more than a guess does. Our page keeps the plumbing side separate so you know who to call about what.

Chicago Heights, ZIP 60411, is an older industrial city where US 30 and Dixie Highway cross. Housing there is often older, so check your home's build year before assuming what the pipes are made of. The page covers drain and sewer problems first, because that is what most calls turn out to be.

Nearby towns we reach by phone

Nearby communities such as Sauk Village, Glenwood, Crete, Flossmoor and Homewood do not have their own pages, but we serve them by calling. Phone the dispatcher at (855) 575-2890, give your address and ask whether a plumber can reach you today.

Why we answer calls instead of forms

We take calls instead of forms because a plumbing problem is usually urgent. A person on the line can ask where the water is coming from, whether the main valve is closed and whether anyone has used the drains since the backup started.

That conversation shapes what the plumber brings to the job, which saves time on site.
