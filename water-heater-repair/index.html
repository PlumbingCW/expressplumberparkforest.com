A water heater fails at the worst hour, usually when the house is full and the garage floor is already wet. We answer the phone 24/7 at (855) 575-2890. While you wait, close the cold supply valve above the tank, turn off the breaker or set the gas control to off, and keep everyone clear of standing water.

Tank leaking or no hot water right now

Water spreading across the floor is the main threat, because it can reach outlets, drywall and stored boxes within minutes. Stop the supply first, then worry about the heater. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and after-hours calls go to the same dispatch line.

  • Shut the cold water valve on the pipe entering the top of the tank. A quarter turn on a ball valve or clockwise on a gate valve.
  • Switch off the dedicated breaker for an electric or hybrid heater. Never leave elements powered with the tank empty.
  • For a gas heater, turn the gas control knob to off. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside before anything else.
  • Move belongings off the floor, and run a hose from the drain valve to a floor drain if water keeps coming.

Is it the tank or just a fitting? How to tell

Not every puddle means the heater is finished. Water that drips from a threaded connection, a drain valve or the relief valve pipe can often be fixed in one visit. Water that seeps out of the jacket or from the very bottom of the steel tank means the inner tank has rusted through.

Where the water appears and what it usually means
Where you see waterLikely causeTypical outcome
Wet pipe threads on topLoose or corroded supply connection or flex lineUsually a repair
Drips from the drain valveWorn valve or sediment holding it openUsually a repair
Discharge pipe from the relief valveExcess pressure or thermal expansion, or a worn valveRepair, and check for an expansion tank
Condensation on the outsideCold incoming water on a humid dayOften harmless and temporary
Water under the tank with a dry topLeak from the inner tankReplace the heater

Wipe the tank dry and watch for ten minutes. A bead that reforms in one spot points to that fitting. Water returning everywhere points to the tank, and a leak hidden in a wall or slab is a job for leak detection.

No hot water, or only lukewarm

The cause depends on the fuel. A gas heater with no heat has usually lost its pilot flame, its thermocouple, its igniter or its gas control valve. An electric heater usually has a failed heating element, a tripped high-limit reset button or a bad thermostat.

Pilot or igniter trouble

If a gas flame will not stay lit, the thermocouple, igniter or gas valve is the usual suspect.

Failed heating element

A bad lower element gives a short supply of hot water. A bad upper one often gives none.

Thermostat or reset trip

The red reset button trips when water overheats, and it keeps tripping until the cause is found.

Broken dip tube

A cracked dip tube lets cold water mix at the top, so hot water runs out early.

Parts that wear out inside a water heater

A steel tank is lined with glass and protected by a sacrificial anode rod. The rod corrodes on purpose so the tank does not. Once it is used up the steel rusts, so checking the rod can add years. The relief valve, drain valve and expansion tank are the other parts that quietly wear out.

A relief valve that drips often signals high pressure in the house piping, not just a tired valve. Replacing the valve alone can hide the real problem. We test the static pressure and look for a missing or waterlogged expansion tank.

Gas, electric and hybrid heaters fail in different ways

What tends to fail on each type
TypeCommon failuresSpecial notes
GasThermocouple, pilot assembly, igniter, gas control valve, blocked flue or draft problemNeeds proper venting. Any gas smell means leave and call the gas utility or 911.
ElectricUpper or lower element, thermostat, high-limit reset, bad wiring connectionPower must be off before anyone opens a panel.
Hybrid (heat pump)Clogged air filter, blocked condensate drain, fan or compressor faults, control errorsNeeds open air space and a drain path for condensate. Backup elements work like an electric tank.

Hybrid units add a heat pump on top of an electric tank, so they can fail in two places. The heat pump side needs a clean filter, a clear condensate line and open air around it.

A gas heater needs combustion air and a clear vent to carry exhaust outside. If one backdrafts or sets off a carbon monoxide alarm, leave the house and call 911 before any repair.

Sediment, flushing and what Park Forest water does to a tank

The Village of Park Forest pumps groundwater from six wells about 300 feet deep, then softens it at the plant with a lime and soda ash process. Softening removes much of the mineral content that bakes into scale on heating surfaces. The Village also adds orthophosphate for corrosion control, which helps protect the pipes that carry water to your house.

That does not make a tank maintenance-free. Orthophosphate works on pipe walls and does nothing to slow the anode rod from wearing out. Fine particles still settle at the bottom of any tank, so we still recommend a sediment check and a flush on a regular schedule, especially if you have a home softener or older plumbing. A flush means draining the tank at the drain valve until the water runs clear.

Repair or replace, a straight way to decide

A heater with a failed part and a healthy tank is a good repair candidate. A heater with a leaking tank is not, no matter how new the part. Most tanks last roughly a decade or somewhat longer, and age is only one signal.

What affects the cost of water heater repair

The failed part

A thermostat, element or flex line is a different job from a gas control valve or a heat pump component.

Fuel type and model

Hybrid and some gas models use parts that cost more and take longer to source.

Access and location

A heater in a tight closet, attic or crawl space takes more time than one in an open garage.

Code or safety corrections

Missing drain pans, relief valve piping, or venting problems may have to be corrected while we are there.

After-hours timing

Night, weekend and holiday visits are a different scheduling situation from a weekday appointment.

Homeowners insurance often responds to sudden water damage from a failed tank but not to the wear-out of the heater itself, so check your policy. Safety steps for a failing water heater

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. Do not flip switches or relight a pilot.

For an electric heater, switch off its breaker before you touch anything near wet wiring. If the tank is leaking, close the cold-water inlet valve on top of the heater and keep children and pets away from hot water.
