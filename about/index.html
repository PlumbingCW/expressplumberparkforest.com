About Park Forest Express Plumbing

We are a 24/7 plumbing dispatch line for Park Forest, Illinois and the nearby villages, sending licensed plumbers to the job.

Call (855) 575-2890

What we do

Park Forest Express Plumbing runs a 24/7 phone line for plumbing problems in Park Forest, Illinois, and the surrounding communities. When you call, a dispatcher listens, asks questions and sends a licensed plumber to your address. Our business address is 200 Main St, Park Forest, IL 60466.

We handle drain and sewer problems, water heaters, leaks, burst pipes, sump pumps and more. The full list is on the services page. We do not work on gas lines, so if you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Where we work

We cover Park Forest itself plus Matteson, Richton Park, Olympia Fields, University Park and Chicago Heights. Our service areas page explains how nearby communities are served by calling. If your town is not listed, call anyway and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether we can reach you.

What happens on a call

You describe the problem

Tell the dispatcher what you see, where the water is and if you have shut it off. The dispatcher will give you safe steps to take while help is on the way.

A plumber is assigned

A licensed plumber is sent to the job. We offer same-day appointments when a technician is open, but we do not promise a specific arrival time.

Diagnosis on site

The plumber looks at the problem, which may involve a camera, a pressure test or a moisture check. Some causes cannot be confirmed without opening a wall or floor.

Price explained first

Before work begins, the plumber explains the findings, the options and what drives the cost. You decide whether to go ahead.

How pricing is explained

We do not quote prices on this website, because plumbing cost depends on access, materials, pipe condition, permits and the time of day. What we do is explain the cost factors before any work starts, so the number you hear is tied to something you can see. If a repair is not worth doing and replacement makes more sense, the plumber will say so.

Park Forest water, in brief

The Village of Park Forest supplies its own water from six wells about 300 feet deep. The Village softens it with lime and soda ash, filters it, and adds chlorine, orthophosphate for pipe corrosion control and fluoride. The Village does not publish a hardness figure on its water page, so we do not quote one.

For homeowners, that means a water test summary comes from the Village Public Works office, not from a plumber. If you want to know what is in your tap water, ask the Village first, then ask us about the pipes and fixtures it passes through.

How to verify any plumber's license

Illinois requires people who install, alter, repair and maintain plumbing to be licensed. Plumber license numbers begin with 058, and plumbing contractors register with the state under numbers that begin with 055. You can look up any plumber on the Illinois Department of Public Health License Verification tool at https://plumblicv5pub.dph.illinois.gov/Clients/ILDOHPlumb/Public/Verification/Plumber_License_Verification.aspx and we encourage you to do that for every contractor you hire.

Park Forest adds its own rule. The Village Building Department states that all contractors doing work in the Village must be Park Forest licensed, and a permit is required if you or a contractor does the work. Ask any plumber, including us, who will pull the permit and what the Village requires.

Where our work stops, and what to do about gas

We cannot diagnose what we cannot reach, and we cannot promise outcomes before a plumber has seen the problem. We do not do gas line work. For anything involving a gas appliance, leave the building if you smell gas and call the gas utility or 911. When you are ready to talk, the contact page has the number and address.
