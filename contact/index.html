Call us any time

Our phone line at (855) 575-2890 is answered 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. We work by phone only, so calling is the way to reach a dispatcher. Same-day appointments are available when a technician is open, and the dispatcher will tell you what is realistic for your address.

Email

We work by phone only and do not have a storefront for walk-in visits. For non-urgent written questions you can email contact@expressplumberparkforest.com, but for an active leak or backup, please call.

Before you call

  • Shut off the main water valve if water is leaking or spraying. It is usually where the water line enters the house.
  • Stop using drains and fixtures if sewage is backing up.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call us about the plumbing.
  • Take photos of the damage for your insurance claim.
  • Have a pen ready and be near the affected area so you can describe what you see.

What to tell the dispatcher

Give your address and the town, since we serve Park Forest, Matteson, Richton Park and nearby villages. Describe the problem in plain words, such as water coming through a ceiling or a toilet that will not stop overflowing. Tell the dispatcher if the water is off, if anyone is in danger and if you rent or own.

It also helps to mention the age of the house, the type of water heater and whether the problem has happened before. We cannot promise an arrival time, but the more detail we have, the better prepared the plumber will be. You can read more about how we work on the about page or browse the services we handle.

Standards and sources

Written by the Park Forest Express Plumbing team. Plumbing work in Illinois follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890, which the Village of Park Forest adopts. The Village Building Department states that contractors must be Park Forest licensed and that a permit is required. You can confirm any plumber's license on the Illinois Department of Public Health license verification tool.
