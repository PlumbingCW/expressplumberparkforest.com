Three subdivisions, one name

Real estate listings in Park Forest group homes under Lincolnwood, East Lincolnwood and West Lincolnwood. All three are single-family areas. Listing-site data puts construction between 1953 and 1969 for East Lincolnwood, 1956 and 1967 for Lincolnwood, and 1961 and 1999 for West Lincolnwood.

Those ranges come from listing sites, not Village records, so treat them as a rough guide. The practical point is simple. A home built in 1958 and one built in the 1990s on the same side of the village can have very different pipes. Tell us the year if you know it.

Drains and sewer backups

The Village flooding page explains that sewers can fill faster than they drain during heavy rain, which can cause sewage backups in basements. It also asks residents not to flush wipes or pour fats and oils down drains. Those habits protect your own line as well as the Village system.

If a basement or floor drain backs up, stop using water and call. A sewer line inspection with a camera shows whether the blockage is a clog, a root or a damaged section. Sewer line cleaning clears the main line from the cleanout when that is the cause.

Safety steps if sewage is coming up

Keep everyone out of the wet area and stop running water. Do not mix drain chemicals into standing sewage. If water is near outlets or the electrical panel, do not step into it, and have the breaker switched off by a licensed electrician or the utility.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Then call us and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from.

Small steps that help

Direct downspouts away from the foundation. Keep wipes and grease out of drains. Test a sump pump by pouring water into the pit before the wet season. If you plan to dig or replace a sewer cleanout, ask the Village Building Department first, since sewer repair permits cover cleanouts.

Questions we hear

We do not have a published street boundary for any of the three subdivisions, so we do not guess. Give the dispatcher your address and we work from that.
