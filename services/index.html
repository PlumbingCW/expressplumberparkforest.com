Plumbing Services in Park Forest, IL

From clogged drains to burst pipes, here is what our licensed plumbers handle across Park Forest and the villages around it.

Call (855) 575-2890

Plumbing problems rarely arrive at a convenient time, so our phone line at (855) 575-2890 is staffed 24/7. The services below cover the situations people phone about most, and each page explains symptoms, methods and what affects the cost.

Water and drain emergencies make up a large share of calls. If a pipe has burst, start with burst pipe repair. For slow or backed-up lines, see drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning. Hidden moisture is a job for leak detection.

Hot water and heating come next. We cover water heater repair, replacement, installation and tankless units, plus boilers. Basement and storm problems, including sump pumps and waterproofing, have their own pages too.

Pick a service from the list, or call if you are not sure which one fits. The dispatcher can sort it out in a couple of minutes, and you can also browse the towns we cover.

What to do first when water is moving

Close the main water shutoff, keep clear of standing water near outlets and appliances, and call the 24/7 line. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber.

Standards and sources

Written by the Park Forest Express Plumbing team. Plumbing work in Illinois follows the Illinois Plumbing Code, 77 Ill. Adm. Code 890, which the Village of Park Forest adopts. The Village Building Department states that contractors must be Park Forest licensed and that a permit is required. You can confirm any plumber's license on the Illinois Department of Public Health license verification tool.
