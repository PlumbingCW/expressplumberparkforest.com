Your water, your pipes, and the questions people ask

University Park's water is supplied by Aqua Illinois, a private utility. In 2017 Aqua switched the village from wells to the Kankakee River, and a treatment change caused some pipes to leach lead. Lead was found in 2019, and the matter went to court. Status may have changed since, so ask Aqua Illinois for current results and test your own tap.

We cannot test water quality ourselves, and an accredited lab is the right route. What we can do is look at your plumbing. We can check what material your service line and interior supply pipes are made of, and explain what a repipe or fixture swap would involve.

Finding a lead service line the practical way

Look where the line enters

Find the pipe coming through the basement or crawl wall near the meter. Lead is dull gray, soft and scratches to a shiny silver.

Use a magnet and a coin

A magnet does not stick to lead or copper. Galvanized steel attracts it. Scratching lightly shows the color underneath.

Ask the utility

Aqua Illinois may hold records for the street side. Ask what they know about your service connection.

Call us for the house side

We confirm the material inside and plan any repiping work to match.

Pipe materials aside, some households flush the tap for a while after the house has been vacant, and a filter rated for lead is a common stopgap. Check a filter's certification before you rely on it.

Water heaters in homes with treated water

Treatment changes can shift how water behaves inside a tank, from scale to corrosion. An anode rod is the sacrificial part that protects the steel shell, and it wears out first. If your tank is old, a water heater repair may only buy time, while water heater replacement restores reliability.

Before We Arrive in University Park

Close the main valve

Water supplied to University Park homes enters at one main line, so one valve controls the whole house. Turn it clockwise and then open a low faucet to drain what remains in the pipes.

Power down wet zones

Before you touch anything near water, flip off the breakers that feed the soaked area. Standing water around a panel or outlet means you wait for us outside the room.

Rescue the valuables

Pull paper files, electronics and rugs clear of the wet floor. A student renting near campus or a family in a larger home can use the same rule, and the dry items will thank you.

Ring the dispatch line

Reach us any hour at (855) 575-2890. Tell us what you see and whether the water looks clear or discolored, so we can send the right tools.
